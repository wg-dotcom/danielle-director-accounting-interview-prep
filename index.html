02 · The business

Know what The Company needs you to build.

The Company is bringing accounting in-house while its products, services, and entity structure become more complex.

  • The Company is a healthcare technology, services, and data business focused on psychiatry.
  • It supports more than 800 independent psychiatry practices across 49 US states.
  • Its revenue spans clinical software, billing and revenue-cycle services, and life-sciences projects.
  • It is a San Francisco-based Series B Public Benefit Corporation launching a new platform.
  • Three entities and several revenue streams create close, intercompany, allocation, and revenue-recognition complexity.

Close and reporting

Own month-end, quarter-end, and year-end close across GL, AP, AR, and payroll for three entities. Deliver accurate US GAAP financials on a reliable timeline.

Multi-entity accounting

Own intercompany transactions, allocations, entity reporting, and the parent-level rollup across the MSO structure.

Technical accounting

Research and document positions, especially ASC 606 across software, revenue-cycle billing, and life-sciences projects.

Transition in-house

Take accounting back from the outsourced provider. Capture knowledge, close documentation gaps, and protect continuity.

Controls and process

Build policies, close procedures, review standards, and clear decision rights.

Systems, automation, and AI

Move from QuickBooks Online Premium to a true multi-entity platform. Redesign the chart of accounts. Use AI where it improves control and speed.

Business partnership

Give Shuchi and leadership clean numbers, clear analysis, and recommendations before they need to ask.

Build first, manage second

Do the work personally and operate with minimal support. Build the team only when the workload justifies it.

03 · Your interviewer

Speak to her like an operator. Lead with the decision, then the evidence.

Who she is

  • VP of Finance with more than 15 years of healthcare-technology finance experience.
  • Owns strategic finance, accounting, capital strategy, investor relations, and the scaling of financial operations.
  • Previously built a finance function from the ground up at a healthcare startup.
  • Earlier experience includes strategic finance in health technology and leadership roles inside Aetna/CVS Health.
  • Chartered Accountant from India with a Master's degree in Applied Financial Mathematics.

How to speak with her

  • Lead with the answer.
  • Name the company and your reporting line.
  • Separate what you prepared from what you reviewed.
  • Give one measurable result.
  • Close with how the lesson applies here.

06 · Pressure test

Answer the concerns directly.

Open each concern. Practice the answer, then replace every general statement with proof.

01Your background is not a traditional formal-accounting path

The truth. Shuchi likes your potential but said formal accounting background is a requirement for the Controller role.

Answer it like this

“I understand the concern. My path is not the traditional CPA path, but the work itself has been formal and hands-on. For nearly nine years, I owned the ledger, close, financial statements, intercompany accounting, payroll, AP, AR, budgets, controls, and audit preparation across a multi-country structure. I can walk you through the close and the judgments behind it.”

Bring this proof. Prepare a complete month-end close example. Name the calendar, reconciliations, entries, financial statements, review points, and final deadline.

02Your recent role was outside accounting

The truth. From November 2023 through June 2026, your work focused on game design, support, and community operations.

Answer it like this

“I took a role outside accounting and learned a great deal about customers, systems, and startup execution. It also confirmed that accounting is the work I want to return to. My Controller foundation spans nearly nine years, and I have prepared to refresh the technical areas this role requires.”

Bring this proof. Give a direct reason for the move and a specific example of how you have refreshed your accounting knowledge.

03Direct ASC 606 ownership is not established

The truth. Your experience shows broad Controller responsibility, but it does not yet prove that you wrote ASC 606 conclusions for software, billing services, or life-sciences projects.

Answer it like this

“I will not overstate direct ASC 606 experience. I understand the method: identify the contract, performance obligations, transaction price, allocation, and timing of recognition; then document the judgment and controls. I would apply it separately to The Company's software, RCM, and project revenue and use technical support where the fact pattern requires it.”

Bring this proof. Prepare one revenue-recognition judgment you did own. Explain the facts, policy, entry, review, and result.

04AI fluency needs a real example

The truth. The role is AI-forward, but your current evidence is stronger in systems implementation and standard automation.

Answer it like this

“I separate AI from automation. My proven experience is selecting systems, migrating data, validating balances, and training users. For AI, I would start with controlled work such as variance triage, reconciliation-exception summaries, procedure drafts, and research drafts. A person would approve every entry and technical conclusion.”

Bring this proof. Bring one workflow with the input, tool, output, human review, data risk, and expected time saved.

05NetSuite is not in your background

The truth. You have Dynamics 365 and QuickBooks experience, not NetSuite.

Answer it like this

“I have not used NetSuite directly. I have led the more important underlying work: requirements, chart-of-accounts design, data migration, intercompany validation, user training, and cutover control. I would learn The Company's current setup first, then evaluate the platform against entity, reporting, control, and integration needs.”

Bring this proof. Review NetSuite multi-entity basics and prepare your migration method from discovery through post-cutover reconciliation.

06Healthcare and MSO accounting are new

The truth. Your Controller experience comes from agriculture and related entities, not healthcare technology or an MSO structure.

Answer it like this

“Healthcare is a new industry for me, so I would not pretend otherwise. The transferable core is multi-entity close, intercompany accounting, controls, system transition, and leadership reporting. I would learn the contracts, entity purpose, billing flows, and regulatory constraints before changing the accounting design.”

Bring this proof. Prepare a 30-day learning plan for The Company's entities, contracts, revenue streams, close calendar, and outsourced-provider handoff.
